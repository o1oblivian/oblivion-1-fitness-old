# Oblivion 1 Fitness Club - System Instructions

Refer to `AGENTS.md` for architectural mandates, color palettes, and Grade A+ engineering standards.
- 100% Genuine single mobile web application.
- Dual-theme support: Light mode default/adaptive, Dark OLED tactical mode (`#09090b` canvas, `#121214` cards, `#C4121A` Oblivion 1 Crimson, `#0284c7` sky).
- Natural organic colors: Zero neon, no electric glows. Pure Oblivion 1 Crimson (`#C4121A`). Sharp high contrast across both Light and Dark themes.
- Zero mock or fake secondary apps.
- Android Manifest Golden Baseline (Version 69): Strictly locked. Permitted permissions ONLY: INTERNET, CAMERA, BLUETOOTH. Never re-introduce READ_MEDIA_*, RECORD_AUDIO, MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS, ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION, or BODY_SENSORS.
- Permanent Rule: Optical Vision Engine must return `null` for non-visible metrics on watches and gym consoles. Never apply zero or estimated fallbacks. Unread metrics must render as '--'.
